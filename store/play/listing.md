## Full description

Can't find your phone? Clap or whistle and Vokey makes it ring, vibrate and flash so you can follow the sound.

HOW IT WORKS
Tap Start Finder before you put your phone down. Vokey listens for your clap, whistle or a supported voice command, then plays your chosen alert. A notification shows while Vokey is listening, with a Stop button always one tap away.

CLAP & WHISTLE DETECTION
• Single, double or triple clap patterns — double and triple claps trigger less by accident
• Whistle detection with adjustable frequency range and minimum length
• Sensitivity sliders and a cooldown between alerts
• Finder Test lets you try your clap and whistle on your own phone

OFFLINE VOICE COMMANDS (OPTIONAL)
Say “Find my phone” or “Where are you?”. Speech is recognised on your phone after a one-time download of the English (US) model. No cloud speech service.

ALERTS THAT GET YOUR ATTENTION
• Gradually rising volume — starts gentle, grows louder
• Built-in tones or import your own sound (MP3, M4A, OGG, WAV, FLAC)
• Vibration and flashlight pulses, including SOS
• Choose the output: automatic, speaker only or headphones only
• Stop, snooze for 30 seconds or mark a false alarm right from the alert

CONTROL AND CONVENIENCE
• Quick Settings tile and home-screen widget to see status and stop listening
• Monitoring Centre with listening time, battery and output status
• Auto-stop after a set time or at a low battery level
• Quiet hours and Pocket Mode to avoid unwanted alerts
• History and detection stats with simple filters

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Sound is analysed in your phone’s memory only — Vokey never records, saves or uploads audio
• Voice commands are never saved or sent anywhere
• No account needed
• Settings and history stay on your phone; clear them any time
• Optional anonymous usage analytics and crash reports (Firebase) can be turned off in Settings

GOOD TO KNOW
• Vokey listens only after you tap Start, and only while its notification is visible.
• Some phone makers limit apps running in the background. If listening stops, check the battery tips in the app.
• Detection works best in a reasonably quiet room. Very loud music or TV can cause missed or extra detections — use Finder Test to tune sensitivity.
• The optional screen-lock shortcut can only lock your phone; it can never unlock it.

Vokey is supported by ads. Ads never appear while Vokey is listening or ringing.
