Privacy Policy

Voice Control & Phone Finder

Effective date: November 5, 2026Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VoiceMate – Voice Control & Phone Finder ("VoiceMate", "we", "our" or "us") handles information when you use the Android application. VoiceMate is designed to process finder audio on your device and does not require an account.

Important microphone notice: VoiceMate starts microphone capture only after you accept the in-app disclosure, deliberately start a microphone feature and grant Android microphone permission. The finder can continue while the app is in the background as an Android microphone foreground service, with an ongoing notification and Stop action. Audio is processed on this device; VoiceMate does not save raw recordings or upload microphone audio.

1. Information processed by VoiceMate

Microphone audio: Android microphone permission (RECORD_AUDIO) is requested when you choose to use a microphone feature. Audio samples are analysed locally in memory for supported sound events. The application does not intentionally store or transmit raw audio recordings.

Detection measurements: The app calculates temporary measurements such as audio level, peak amplitude, noise estimate and frequency. These measurements are used locally by the detection algorithms and are not uploaded to a VoiceMate server.

Local app preferences and history: Settings such as alert preferences, voice-command choices and onboarding state are stored on your device. The event history stores event type, time and limited numeric metadata (for example, a detected frequency or count). The history is limited to the most recent 250 events. You can clear it in Settings. This data is not sent to a VoiceMate backend.

Offline voice model: If you choose to enable offline voice commands, the app downloads the English (US) Vosk model once over HTTPS and stores it in the app's private storage. The download request is sent to the model host and may expose normal network metadata such as IP address and request headers to that host. Once installed, recognition and supported command matching happen on the device without a speech-to-cloud service. VoiceMate does not store transcripts or upload audio.

Device and battery information: The Battery Centre reads charge level, charging state, reported battery health, temperature, voltage and Android power-saver state. These readings are displayed locally and are not uploaded.

Optional screen lock: If you enable the optional Voice Screen Lock, Android asks you to activate a Device Admin permission that allows the app to lock the device. The feature only requests the lock action; it cannot unlock the device or bypass your PIN, pattern, password or biometrics.

Information we do not ask for: VoiceMate does not require your name, email address, phone number, contacts, SMS, call history, precise location or account credentials for its core finder features.

2. When the microphone is active

  • Microphone capture starts only after an intentional action in the app and the required Android permission.
  • Active finder monitoring runs as an Android microphone foreground service and displays an ongoing notification with a Stop action.
  • Audio is processed on the device and is not intended to be retained as a recording.
  • You can stop an active test or finder mode in the app. You can also revoke microphone permission in Android settings.

Detection performance depends on your device microphone, microphone placement, Android version, surroundings and noise. VoiceMate does not guarantee that every clap, whistle or voice command will be detected.

3. Advertising and third-party services

VoiceMate integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). UMP refreshes consent information on app launch, presents any required consent form, and the app checks canRequestAds() before requesting an ad. The current layout uses a banner on the Home screen only while finder monitoring is off. Debug builds use Google's test banner unit. Release banner requests remain disabled until a real AdMob banner unit ID is configured. When ads are requested, Google and its advertising services may automatically process information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general or approximate location;
  • App and ad interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views;
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as crash, hang or energy-use information;
  • Device and advertising identifiers, such as Android Advertising ID and app-set identifiers, where available and permitted.

These data may be used for advertising, ad measurement, analytics, fraud prevention and security. Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. Data processing and personalization depend on the SDK version, configuration, device settings, your choices and applicable law.

Where required, VoiceMate uses Google's User Messaging Platform to present privacy choices and provides an in-app entry point to reopen the privacy options form. You can review Google's privacy information at Google Privacy Policy and advertising information at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

We do not control the independent privacy practices of third-party providers. Before release, this section and the Play Store Data safety form must be checked against the exact advertising SDK version and configuration shipped in the app.

4. How information is used

VoiceMate uses locally processed audio to detect supported sound events and commands, local preferences to remember your chosen settings, and local event metadata to show your detection history. The current development build does not include advertising SDKs. We do not sell microphone recordings or use them to build advertising profiles.

5. Sharing and disclosure

VoiceMate does not send microphone audio or local finder preferences to a VoiceMate-operated server. When UMP permits and a production banner unit ID is configured, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may send advertising-related information to Google and its service providers under Google's service and privacy terms. The optional speech-model installer sends a download request to the model host over HTTPS; that host can receive normal connection metadata. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect the security and rights of users and the service.

6. Storage and retention

App preferences, the optional offline speech model and event history are stored in VoiceMate's app-private storage. Event history is capped at 250 records and can be cleared in Settings. Android removes the app-private data when you uninstall the app. The speech model can be removed by clearing VoiceMate's app storage. Advertising data retention is governed by Google and its partners' applicable privacy terms and controls.

7. Security

We design VoiceMate to minimise the information it handles. Microphone processing is local, and the app does not maintain a VoiceMate account database or cloud audio store. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely risk-free. Third-party advertising traffic is handled under the provider's own security controls.

8. Children's privacy

VoiceMate is a general utility and is not designed or marketed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through a VoiceMate account or backend. The app's target-audience and advertising settings must be configured consistently with the actual release and Google Play Families requirements if children are included in the intended audience.

9. Your choices

  • Grant or revoke microphone permission using Android system settings.
  • Stop microphone testing or monitoring using the in-app Stop control or the monitoring notification when available.
  • Change local preferences in VoiceMate or clear VoiceMate's app storage/uninstall the app to remove locally stored settings.
  • Use Android and Google advertising privacy controls to manage advertising identifiers and ad personalization where available.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features, permissions, advertising SDKs or legal requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page will show when the policy was last revised. Material changes will be communicated in the app or through the app's store listing where appropriate.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the developer contact details shown on the VoiceMate Google Play Store listing. Those contact details must be published and monitored before the app is released.
